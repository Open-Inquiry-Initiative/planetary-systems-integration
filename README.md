# Planetary Systems Integration (PSI-001)

**Open Inquiry Initiative | Public research preview v0.1.0 | 13 November 2026**

> **Status: Conceptual hypothesis, not a validated engineering design.** No claimed performance improvements have been experimentally demonstrated.

## Why this exists
An exploratory human–AI engineering discussion started with an extreme question about scaling lithography equipment and followed the physical dependencies outward: precision manufacturing → thermal management → waste-heat cascades and exergy → distributed infrastructure → integrated city and planetary planning.

The resulting research question is: **Can coordinated planning across infrastructure layers outperform independent subsystem optimization while preserving reliability, ecological boundaries and human welfare?**

This repository publishes a **curated, reconstructable discovery record**, not a raw private conversation or a claim of a breakthrough. Existing fields such as systems-of-systems engineering, district energy, industrial symbiosis, exergy analysis, GIS and digital twins inform the concept.

## Layer model
L00 Physical constraints; L01 Energy; L02 Thermal and exergy; L03 Water; L04 Materials and manufacturing; L05 Built environment and transportation; L06 Human and ecological systems; L07 Coordination and fault isolation; L08 Validation and independent review.

## How to reproduce or challenge the idea
Implement two models of the **same synthetic region**, with identical weather, service requirements and resource limits: A optimizes individual subsystems; B coordinates across them. Compare energy, water, costs, emissions, resilience and service outcomes; enforce conservation and exergy constraints; publish negative findings. **No simulation code or measured outcome is included in this first release.**
